The privacy of this page

This page collects no data.

It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it asks you for no data.

Fonts, images and code come from this same site: your browser calls no outside service. The “Third-party requests” box at the top of the product page counts them.

The light or dark theme you choose is saved only in your browser.

The page is published with GitHub Pages. Like any server, GitHub’s receives the address of your connection when you open the page, and handles it under its own privacy statement.

When you download the program, the file comes from the releases published on GitHub.

brAIn and your Google account data

Up to here, this page is about this website. This part is about the brAIn program, when you connect it to your Google account.

You connect from your computer, on Google’s own sign-in screen, and you choose which permissions to give. brAIn uses Google data only for the features you see in the program:

  • Gmail: it reads your messages to show them in Mail. Only if you turn the matching permissions on, it writes drafts and sends the messages you approve, or labels, archives and moves messages to the trash.
  • Calendar: it reads your calendars and their events to show them in Calendar; at most 25 events of your primary calendar enter the program’s memory.
  • Drive: it reads the list of your files (name, type, date), never their content; at most 25 file records enter the program’s memory.

Mail never enters the program’s memory on its own: only a message you save does, one at a time.

Google data stays on your computer, with the access keys Google gives you. It never goes through a server of ours, we never see it, we never sell it, and we never use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Only you see it, and the AI models you connect and choose, when you use an AI feature on your mail, calendar or files, or when a routine you set up does. The model provider’s privacy policy applies to that data too.

You can disconnect Google whenever you want, from Integrations: brAIn stops reading and deletes the access keys from your computer. You can also remove its access on the permissions page of your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions

What you saved into the program’s memory stays on your computer until you delete it.

brAIn's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
