The AI assistants you already have. Finally working for you.

A program on your computer that puts them to work on your documents and tells you what it did.

Try itPrivate alpha

They answer brilliantly. But none of them really works for you.

Every time they start from zero, and you explain the context again. A long job needs you watching every step. What you spent, you find out later. And your documents end up on someone else’s servers.

Ask. It opens the files, searches, prepares, and shows you every step.

You tell it the result, not the command.

First it asks the right questions. Then it writes the plan, works in parallel, checks the results and signs a receipt of what it did.

While it works you can pause it or tell it something. You stay the boss.

  1. The questions

    Before spending anything, it makes sure of what you really want.

  2. The plan

    Goals, parts and tasks, with the expected cost. You approve it.

  3. The waves

    Several assistants work together, each on its own part.

  4. The check

    Every result is checked before it calls itself done.

  5. The receipt

    At the end it signs a proof of what it did, and with which files.

A memory that stays, and that you can look at.

Documents, notes, people, projects and conversations become one map. Whoever works for you starts there, not from zero.
